## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an emotional sanctuary for digital legacy, remembrance, and intergenerational transfer. Leaving one's final wishes and memories should feel like sealing a handwritten letter inside a quiet, sunlit room, not executing a sterile legal transaction or configuring a cloud database. The aesthetic bridges the nostalgia of a vintage train carriage peering over expansive countryside landscapes with the crisp reassurance of modern archival security.

The visual direction marries **Warm Editorial Classicism** with **Muted Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- **Tone:** Reverent, grounded, deeply human, secure, and serene.
- **Atmosphere:** Creamy vellum backdrops, tactile mineral borders, delicate organic blurs that mimic frosted carriage glass, and earthy accents of terracotta, meadow green, and deep teal.
- **Anti-Patterns:** Strictly avoid high-tech cryptographic tropes (matrix grids, glowing neon circuits, aggressive dark terminal aesthetics), harsh cold grays, and hyper-caffeinated SaaS micro-interactions.

## Colors

The palette is derived from a cinematic journey through open country, anchored by timeless material contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#163B3D` - Deep Teal):** The anchor of trust and gravitas. Used for primary interactive actions, high-importance callouts, high-contrast headings, and foundational branding.
- **Secondary (`#A9573E` - Terracotta):** The human heart of the platform. Conveys warmth, legacy, wax seals, and intimate milestones. Used for emotional accents, subtle highlights, active states, and warm notification markers.
- **Tertiary (`#78965E` - Meadow Green):** Natural renewal and ongoing life. Reserved for positive affirmations, confirmed inheritances, verified status badges, and soothing progress states.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F5F2E9` - Warm Ivory):** The tactile ground plane. Replaces stark modern whites with a warm, archival linen feel that reduces eye fatigue and inspires pause.
- **Sky Blue (`#9BCBE8`):** Atmosphere, breath, and horizon. Utilized primarily in translucent glass tints, gentle informational badges, and ambient radial gradients.
- **Charcoal (`#263238`):** Deep editorial ink for longform reading typography and fine structural outlines.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated glass cards, input surface fills, and crisp modal dialogs to preserve clarity against the ivory canvas.

### Color Distribution Rules
- 65% Warm Ivory canvas base with subtle ambient Sky Blue/Ivory light pools.
- 20% Deep Teal and Charcoal for crisp typographic hierarchy and solid interactive anchors.
- 10% Translucent White/Glass surfaces for layered cards and modals.
- 5% Terracotta and Meadow Green for purposeful emotional and state accents.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic contemplation and crisp geometric modernism:
- **Headlines (`Newsreader`):** A literary, transitional serif with warmth and grace. It elevates will creation and memory preservation into an enduring rite of passage. It handles all introspective storytelling, major view titles, and emotional prompts. It features gentle italic expressions for introspective subheadings.
- **Body & Controls (`Outfit`):** A clean, warm geometric sans-serif that echoes the open proportions of contemporary design while keeping characters open and soft. It delivers effortless legibility across complex asset distribution forms, identity verification protocols, and key legal fine print.

### Editorial Guidelines
- Use italics in `Newsreader` headlines selectively for singular evocative nouns or verbs (e.g., *Letters* to Tomorrow, The *Keepers*).
- Maintain generous line-heights across body text to preserve a restful, paced cadence for deep reading.
- Uppercase tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.04em - 0.06em`) is restricted to tiny semantic labels, status indicators, and legal step tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **composed architectural grid** with unhurried margins, providing generous negative space to minimize cognitive burden and emotional anxiety.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout. Max container width capped at `1240px` to maintain intimate reading measures. Gutters sit at `1.5rem` (`24px`), with outer margins of `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout. Gutters at `1.25rem` (`20px`), margins at `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutters at `1rem` (`16px`), margins at `1.25rem` (`20px`).

### Spatial Rhythm
- Spatial padding between sections scales deliberately using increments of `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) and multiples thereof.
- Longform legacy documents and inventory forms are centered in a focused 8-column column measure (max `720px`) to prevent visual drifting.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated as atmospheric layers of frosted glass, warm ivory vellum, and soft ambient light, avoiding heavy, dark industrial shadows.

### Atmospheric Glassmorphism
- **Base Canvas:** Solid `#F5F2E9` (Warm Ivory) with subtle radial gradients of `#9BCBE8` (Sky Blue at 12% opacity) pooled in distant corners to resemble daylight through a train window.
- **Glass Surfaces (Cards & Floating Navigation):** Semi-opaque white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), backed by a hardware-accelerated `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`.
- **Borders & Outlines:** Low-contrast double-rim borders using `rgba(22, 59, 61, 0.08)` (faint Deep Teal) paired with an inner top specular rim (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).

### Ambient Shadows
- **Level 1 (Embedded / Resting Panels):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(22, 59, 61, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(22, 59, 61, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Context Panels):** `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(22, 59, 61, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(22, 59, 61, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Pill Navigation & Modals):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(22, 59, 61, 0.12), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(22, 59, 61, 0.06)`. All shadows use deep teal undertones rather than pure black, ensuring organic unity with the ivory ground.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, softened geometry that feels natural to touch and hold:
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to form fields, badge containers, item thumbnails, and structural table wrappers.
- **Card Containers (`1rem` / 16px):** Used for archival cards, asset groupings, testimonial windows, and insight drawers.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Used for legacy vaults, will-reading viewports, and credential dialogs.
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Reserved for high-tactility elements: the floating bottom/top global navigation bar, action buttons, filter tags, and signature validation toggles.

## Components

### Floating Pill Navigation
- Centered horizontally, fixed to either top or bottom viewport edge with `1.5rem` inset.
- Built on a glass surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`, `backdrop-blur: 20px`) with an overall border-radius of `9999px`.
- Enclosed by a 1px border of `rgba(22, 59, 61, 0.1)`.
- Active items use a pill pillion highlight in Deep Teal (`#163B3D`) with White text, while inactive items use muted Charcoal (`#263238`) hovering into Warm Ivory tinted backdrops.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Teal (`#163B3D`) background with Warm Ivory (`#F5F2E9`) text. Fully rounded pill (`roundedness: 3` / 9999px). On hover, transitions gently to a deepened spruce teal (`#0E2728`) with Level 2 elevation.
- **Emotional / Secondary:** Terracotta (`#A9573E`) background with White (`#FFFFFF`) text. Reserved for irreversible commitments, emotional milestone actions ("Seal Document", "Record Voice Memory"), and primary progress confirmations.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent surface with a 1px border in `rgba(22, 59, 61, 0.2)` and text in Deep Teal. On hover, fills with `rgba(245, 242, 233, 0.6)`.

### Cards & Legacy Containers
- Structured with `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) geometry, translucent White backgrounds (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), and fine subtle outlines (`1px solid rgba(22, 59, 61, 0.08)`).
- Cards feature comfortable interior padding (`space-lg` / `1.5rem`).
- When cards hold deeply personal artifacts (letters, photo records), the header features Newsreader italic titles accompanied by a subtle Meadow Green or Sky Blue status stamp.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp White background (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure clear contrast against the Warm Ivory page. Bordered with a delicate `1px solid rgba(38, 50, 56, 0.18)` and `0.5rem` corners. Active focus states ring with a soft Sky Blue halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(155, 203, 232, 0.45)`) and Deep Teal stroke.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved custom elements using Deep Teal active fills with a White checkmark. Focus states mirror the soft Sky Blue halo.
- **Chips & Tags:** Rounded pill shape, populated with Meadow Green tints (`rgba(120, 150, 94, 0.12)`) and green typography (`#4F673B`) for verified assets, or Terracotta tints (`rgba(169, 87, 62, 0.12)`) for critical attention reminders.

### Specialized Inheritance Components
- **The "Wax Seal" Notary Badge:** A circular or pill medallion using low-saturation Terracotta and subtle debossed inner shadows, indicating encrypted, tamper-proof will clauses.
- **Beneficiary Trust Cards:** Segmented cards highlighting designated recipients, paired with avatar rings tinted in Sky Blue, Meadow Green, and Deep Teal to gracefully differentiate roles (Executor, Trustee, Guardian).
- **Time-Capsule Timeline:** A continuous vertical rail rendered in muted Sky Blue with terracotta nodes, framing memories, video transfers, and conditional triggers in clear chronological flow.
